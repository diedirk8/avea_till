The only goal
After you finish, the closing balance on the FNB statement and the balance in Avea (after things that have not hit the bank yet) must be the same. That figure is called Difference. It must be R0.00.

1. What “reconciled” actually means

The FNB statement is the truth of the bank account. Avea is the shop’s books. Reconciliation is the monthly job of proving they tell the same story.

Card sales take a detour, which is why this feels harder than cash:

  1. A customer pays on the till with Card.
  2. Yoco or Capitec takes that sale, keeps a fee (and Yoco often keeps a loan repayment), then pays the rest into FNB a day later.
  3. FNB shows a Magtape Credit Yoco or Magtape Credit Capitec — smaller than the till, and mixed in with salaries, suppliers, and everything else.

Until you settle those deposits, Avea still thinks the card money is “outstanding receipts” waiting to be banked. That is why the Bank balance in Avea can look nothing like the FNB app.

You are not balancing the till here
Cash-up is a different job. This guide is only the FNB current account. Cash, petty cash, and the safe have their own balances under Money → Account Balances.

2. Gather three files every month

Download all three for the same calendar month before you open Avea.

File Where to get it What it proves
FNB Gold Business statement PDF FNB App or Online Banking → Statements. Digital PDF, not a photo of a printout. Every rand in and out of 62920038462. This is the statement Avea ticks.
Yoco payouts CSV Yoco portal → Payouts → export CSV for the statement month (include a day either side if the last payout lands on the 1st). How much of each Yoco deposit is sales, fee, and capital.
Capitec merchant statement PDF Capitec Merchant Portal (merchant.capitecbank.co.za) → statements. Check the statement period, not the issued date. How much of each Capitec deposit is sales versus commission.
Issued date is not the period
The Capitec file named monthly_statement_2026-08-06 was issued on 6 August, but the period printed on page 1 was 2 Jul 2026 – 1 Aug 2026. That is a July statement. It will not match August FNB deposits. Always read “Statement period” on page 1 before you attach the file.

If you attach a Yoco CSV or Capitec merchant PDF as the FNB statement, Avea will stop you and tell you to put it under Card machine files instead.

3. The Yoco trick everyone misses

On FNB you only see the amount that landed. On the Yoco CSV that amount is the Amount column. It is already after fees and after capital. Do not type that number as “card sales”.

Yoco column What it is Example (1 Aug 2026)
Amount What FNB received (Magtape Credit Yoco) R 3,062.51
Fee amount + Fee VAT Yoco’s card fee. Books as Bank Charges. R 112.50
Capital Repayment Repayment of the Yoco facility. A loan, not a sale and not a fee. R 602.29
Implied card sales Amount + fee + VAT + capital. This is what the till sold on Yoco that batch. R 3,777.30
Why this matters
If you treat the whole gap as a “card fee”, August 2026 would overstate fees by R 14,324.87 — that was Yoco capital, not commission. Avea now splits it automatically when the CSV is attached.

Capitec is simpler: the merchant statement has gross machine totals and net payouts. The difference is commission + VAT. Capitec does not take a capital repayment off the payout.

4. Do the recon, step by step

Work is saved as you go. You can close the screen and reopen it from Money → Bank reconciliations.

1

Open a new reconciliation

In Avea go to Money → New bank reconciliation. You can also open Money → Bank reconciliations and press New reconciliation.

You need the manager role. The screen starts in Draft.

2

Choose the bank account

Set Bank account to Bank — the FNB Gold Business account. Do not pick Store Credit or a cash journal.

3

Attach the FNB statement

Under FNB statement (PDF, CSV, or OFX) upload the Gold Business PDF for the month. CSV or OFX from FNB also works if you ever export those instead.

4

Attach the card machine files

Under Card machine files click to add a row, then attach:

  • the Yoco payouts CSV, and
  • the Capitec merchant statement PDF for this month.

You can add both. If you only have Yoco today, attach Yoco and continue — Capitec deposits will stay unmatched until you add the right Capitec file and press Refresh matches.

5

Click Load & match

The orange Load & match button is in the header, top left — not at the bottom of the form. After it runs, the status becomes In progress.

Avea will:

  • Read every FNB line (salaries, suppliers, Yoco, Capitec, fees, card spend).
  • Rename Yoco and Capitec magtape lines to “card settlement (sales …)”.
  • Fill opening and closing balances from the PDF.
  • Match machine payouts to those deposits so you do not type batch totals.
  • Suggest a booking on ordinary expenses and income (salary, bank charges, fuel, and so on).
6

Read the two scoreboards before you click anything else

Does it balance? is the bank vs the books:

FieldMeaning
Balance in AveaWhat the Bank ledger says at the statement end date.
Not on this statement yetThings already in Avea that this FNB PDF does not show (they stay outstanding).
Avea after those itemsBooks adjusted for those outstanding items.
DifferenceStatement closing balance minus that adjusted figure. Must end at R0.00.

Do the card machines balance? is till vs machines vs bank:

FieldMeaning
Till card salesEverything taken as Card on the till in this period.
Card sales on the machinesGross from the attached Yoco and Capitec files.
Till vs machinesIf this is large, a machine file is usually the wrong month or missing.
Card money in the bankYoco + Capitec deposits on FNB.
Card fees / Yoco capital repaidTaken off the payouts. Fees go to Bank Charges. Capital goes to Loans.

If a yellow warning says the Capitec file has no payouts on this statement, stop and attach the correct month. Then Refresh matches.

7

Settle the card batches in one click

When machine files matched deposits, the header shows Settle card batches. Click it once.

For every matched Yoco and Capitec deposit Avea posts:

  • the net amount into Bank (what FNB actually received),
  • the fee (incl. VAT) to Bank Charges,
  • Yoco capital to Loans (not to fees),
  • the full card sales off Outstanding Receipts.

Those lines jump to status Matched. You should not type 22 or 44 batch totals by hand.

If a Capitec deposit is still unmatched, it has no machine payout. Either attach the right Capitec PDF and refresh, or open that one line and use Settle to type the machine total from the merchant statement.

8

Work every remaining unmatched line

Open the Bank statement tab. Each row is one FNB line. Spent left the account. Received came in. Status must become Matched on every row before you can complete.

Use the next section as the decision tree. Typical August lines:

  • Salaries and Payshap — Record as salary / wages.
  • Supplier payments (Montego, Minitra, Biofarm, Qualipet, …) — Record as expense, or Match to the bill if you already captured it.
  • FNB App Payment From Cash / ADT Cash Deposit — Match as a Transfer from Cash.
  • Payment To Investment Dep / Internet Trf From Dep — Transfer to or from that other account.
  • Blank or tiny debit with no shop name — FNB service fee. Record as bank charge.
  • POS Purchase Netflix, Roblox, Checkers, Amazon, Takealot — personal or mixed spend on the business card. Record as an expense or drawings. Do not leave them hanging.

Where Avea already found the same amount in the books, the status is Possible match. Click Accept on the line, or Accept possible matches in the header if you have checked they are right.

9

Check “Missing from bank”

This tab lists payments already in Avea that this statement does not show. They are allowed. They stay outstanding until a later statement clears them (for example a transfer you made on the 31st that only appears on September’s PDF).

On your first completed recon, older book entries are treated as part of the opening balance unless you turn on Older entries are still missing from the bank. Leave that off unless you know a specific old cheque or transfer has still not hit FNB.

10

Difference must be R0.00

When every statement line is Matched, look at Difference again. If it is not zero, you still have a booking wrong, a duplicate, or an opening balance that does not follow from last month.

Complete reconciliation will refuse to finish until Difference is zero and nothing is still Unmatched.

11

Complete, then stop

Press Complete reconciliation. The record locks as Completed. Next month, start a new one. Do not reopen a completed month to “fix a line” — that is how balances fork. If you started on the wrong files, press Cancel before completing, then start again.

5. What to do with each remaining line

Read the description, then pick one action. Never skip a line.

If you see… Click Then
Status Possible match, and the linked entry is the right one Accept Done. Avea already had this payment.
Yoco or Capitec card settlement, machine file attached Settle card batches (header) Prefer the header button over settling one by one.
Yoco or Capitec card settlement, no machine file Settle Type card sales before the fee. For Yoco also type capital repaid. Fee is calculated.
Money out: supplier, salary, SARS, insurance, rent, fuel, shopping Record Paid to, account (Avea suggests one), amount (already filled). Record Expense.
Money in that is not a card batch (customer EFT, cash dumped, refund) Record or Match Record as income, or Match to an open invoice.
Money moving between Bank and Cash / Safe / another bank Match → Transfer Choose the other account. The other side stays outstanding until you recon that account.
One payment that is part bill, part something else Match → Split Split across accounts. Amounts must add up to the bank line.
Personal POS purchase on the business card Record Expense or directors’ drawings. Do not pretend it is stock.
Do not import to suspense
There is no “just dump it in a holding account and finish”. Skip and suspense import are blocked on purpose. Every line must be a real expense, income, transfer, invoice match, or card settlement.

8. Monthly checklist

Print this page and tick it on statement day.

Task
☐Download the FNB Gold Business PDF for the month (digital, not a scan).
☐Export Yoco payouts CSV covering the month.
☐Download Capitec merchant statement and confirm the period is this month.
☐Money → New bank reconciliation → Bank → attach all three → Load & match.
☐Read “Do the card machines balance?”. If Till vs machines is huge, fix the files first.
☐Settle card batches.
☐Accept possible matches you agree with.
☐Record or Match every leftover spent and received line (salaries, suppliers, cash, personal card).
☐Glance at Missing from bank — outstanding items are fine if they really have not hit FNB.
☐Difference is R0.00. Complete reconciliation.
☐Put the three source files in the month’s folder so next month you know what you used.

9. If something looks wrong

What you seeWhat to do
“This is a Yoco/Capitec card-machine file, not the FNB bank statement.” You attached the machine file in the statement slot. Put the Gold Business PDF there; machines go under Card machine files.
Warning that the Capitec/Yoco file has no payouts on this statement Wrong month. Replace the file, then Refresh matches.
Settle card batches is missing No deposits have a matching machine payout yet. Attach files and Refresh. Check Amounts on the CSV equal the FNB credits.
Till vs machines is tens of thousands Usually one machine file missing or July-vs-August. The till has one Card button for both machines, so the till total will only agree when both files are for this month.
Complete says Difference is not zero An unmatched line remains, a line was booked at the wrong amount, or opening balance does not follow the previous completed statement. Do not force it.
Complete says a line is already cleared on another recon That book entry was used on a previous (or cancelled-then-reposted) recon. Do not link it twice.
You started July five times and cancelled Cancel leaves the books untouched. Start once with the three files, settle cards, then grind the other lines. Cancelling does not fix Outstanding Receipts — only settling the deposits does.
Bank in Avea is wildly unlike FNB even after a month is completed Older months were never cleared. Work backwards from the oldest uncompleted statement, or ask your accountant to set the Bank opening balance and Outstanding Receipts before you recon the current month. Going forward, one completed recon per month keeps it honest.
Rhythm that works
Same three downloads, same eleven clicks, once a month, as soon as FNB issues the statement. The card machines are the noisy part — once they are settled, the rest is ordinary spent and received, one line at a time.
