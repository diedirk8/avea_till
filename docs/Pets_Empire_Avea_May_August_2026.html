September — you do this one

Opening bank is the 31 August close: R 9,873.06. You already have Yoco (the big CSV) and the Capitec merchant statement issued 4 Oct 2026 (2 Sep – 1 Oct trading). The only file still to download is FNB Gold Business statement 60 (September 2026).

  • FNB statement 60 must open at R 9,873.06. If it does not, stop and send it here before you tick anything.
  • Attach FNB 60 + Yoco CSV + Capitec issued 4 Oct 2026.
  • The 1 September Yoco payout sits on September, not August.
  • Capitec settlements dated 1 Sep may be on this FNB month even though they sit on the August merchant file — that is normal period split.
  • Difference must be R 0.00 before Complete reconciliation.
May in Avea
Do not load the whole of FNB statement 56 as May in Avea. Lines dated 1–12 May are already in the pre-Odoo books. Only 13 May onward is the first Avea month. If the wizard will not split a PDF, set the recon period to 13–31 May 2026 and skip or exclude the earlier lines.
