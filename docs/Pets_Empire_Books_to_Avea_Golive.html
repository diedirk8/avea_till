In one paragraph

Nobody typed 2025 into QuickBooks, and Avea did not exist yet. The bank still ran every day. So the books for this gap are the FNB statement, with Yoco and Capitec opened up so card sales show at the till amount, not the smaller amount that landed in the bank. On the morning Avea went live the current account had R 4,760.98. That is the opening bank figure Avea should carry.

What this is
A complete cash book for FNB Gold Business, mapped onto the same accounts QuickBooks already used (Sales, Cost of Sales, Salaries, Rent, Yoco Lening, Xalindri, Savings, Drawings). Every rand that went through this account between the cutoff and go-live is in here.
What this is not
It is not a Lightspeed till recon, and it is not a full stock or VAT return. Cash still in the till, supplier invoices not yet paid, and VAT on those invoices are not on the FNB statement. Those stay unknown until you pull Lightspeed / supplier paper. Amounts are as they hit the bank (VAT-inclusive where the supplier charged VAT).

Opening figure for Avea
On 13 May 2026, before the first Avea till day, FNB Gold Business held R 4,760.98. Use that as the opening bank balance in Avea. Do not use the QuickBooks register figure of R 47,913.83 — that is uncleared later entries, not the bank.

5. What Avea should do from 13 May 2026

  • Leave QuickBooks frozen at 30 September 2024.
  • Set Avea’s FNB opening to R 4,760.98 at 13 May 2026.
  • From 13 May onward, tick each month in Money → Bank reconciliations with that month’s FNB PDF, Yoco CSV and Capitec merchant statement.
  • May 2026 FNB statement 56 covers 30 April – 31 May. Only lines dated 13 May onward belong in Avea. The 1–12 May lines are already in these books.
